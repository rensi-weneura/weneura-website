THE CHALLENGE & SOLUTION

Why Generic ERPs Fail on the Shopfloor

Most manufacturing plants juggle disconnected spreadsheets, standalone accounting tools, and off-the-shelf software packages that only half-fit their real processes. Storekeepers re-type figures, dispatch logs lag behind production, inventory counts on screen don’t match physical stock on pallets, and plant heads make decisions using outdated information.

At Weneura Futuretech, we engineer custom ERP software built around how your business actually runs. Because our team also works directly in robotics, sensors, and factory automation, our ERP connects straight to your weighbridge scales, digital counters, and machine PLCs. You get real-time shopfloor visibility, full source code and database ownership, and zero monthly per-seat licensing penalties as your team expands.

ENGINEERED CAPABILITIES

Modules Built for Manufacturing Scale

Every module is tailored to your exact bill of materials, routing sequences, and reporting workflows.

Production & Routing

Multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM), work orders, job cards, machine stage tracking, operator dispatch logs, and scrap/rework analysis.

Inventory & Warehousing

Multi-warehouse stock, raw materials, WIP, and finished goods tracking. Lot/batch traceability, barcode/QR generation, and auto reorder triggers.

GST Accounts & Billing

Automated GST e-invoicing and e-way bill generation, job-work costing, receivables, payables, and live financial statements.

Procurement & Vendors

Purchase requisitions, vendor RFQs, purchase orders, and direct Goods Receipt Note (GRN) logging linked with digital weigh scales.

Quality Control & Traceability

Inward material inspections, stage-gate tolerance checklists, non-conformance logs, and end-to-end heat number traceability.

Dashboards & Live Telemetry

Role-based dashboards for plant managers, shift supervisors, and accountants. Live OEE indicators and exportable audit reports.

CLARIFICATIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom ERP development take?

A standard Phase 1 MVP release takes 8 to 12 weeks. Core modules like inventory, purchase orders, and shopfloor routing are launched first so your teams can start benefiting immediately. Additional modules are delivered in incremental sprints.

Can you migrate our existing Tally and Excel data?

Yes. We systematically clean, map, and import historical items, vendor masters, customer records, and ledger balances from Excel spreadsheets, Tally ERP, and legacy relational databases prior to go-live.

Can it connect to our weighbridge and factory machines?

Yes. Unlike traditional web software firms, Weneura specializes in factory hardware and automation. We integrate digital weigh scales, barcode/RFID scanners, and machine PLCs directly with your ERP database.

Who owns the software and data?

You do. You receive 100% intellectual property ownership of the source code, database architecture, and documentation upon project handover, with zero recurring per-user licensing fees.

Can our operators use the system on handhelds or mobile phones?

Yes. The user interfaces are fully responsive and touch-optimized for rugged barcode scanners, warehouse tablets, and mobile phones, enabling live dispatch and stock verification anywhere in your facility.

Ready to modernize your manufacturing operations?

Tell us how your factory runs today. Our engineering team will review your shopfloor workflows and provide a clear modular architecture, delivery roadmap, and transparent quote.
