Odoo ERP Development Process

Our proven step-by-step methodology to design, build, and deploy high-performance Odoo solutions.

01

Business Analysis & Requirement Gathering

We analyze your operational workflows, departmental pain points, and map existing spreadsheets to define your exact Odoo roadmap.

02

Planning & Solution Design

We architect the complete system blueprint, selecting Community vs. Enterprise apps, database schemas, and integration points.

03

Odoo Configuration & Customization

We configure standard Odoo modules—Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Accounting—adapting tax rules, currencies, and approval tiers.

04

Module Development & Integration

We develop bespoke Python modules and XML views, while connecting external APIs, weigh scales, barcode tools, and factory PLCs.

05

Data Migration & Validation

We clean, format, and securely migrate your legacy master data from Tally, Excel sheets, and older databases with complete validation.

06

Testing & Quality Assurance

We conduct end-to-end functional testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), stress benchmarks, and security audits across all devices.

07

Deployment & User Training

We launch the system on dedicated cloud or on-premise servers and train your staff, operators, and managers for smooth adoption.

08

Ongoing Support & Optimization

We provide continuous SLA maintenance, server monitoring, regular data backups, performance tuning, and future version upgrades.
