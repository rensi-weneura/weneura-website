Custom ERP Development

One unified ERP engineered around how your manufacturing plant and enterprise actually run. From multi-warehouse inventory, shopfloor machine tracking, and GST accounting to direct weigh scale and PLC integration—with 100% intellectual property ownership and zero recurring per-seat licensing penalties.

Schedule an Engineering Call Explore All Services

Why Generic ERPs Fail on the Shopfloor

Most manufacturing plants juggle disconnected spreadsheets, standalone accounting tools, and off-the-shelf software packages that only half-fit their real processes. Storekeepers re-type figures, dispatch logs lag behind production, inventory counts on screen don’t match physical stock on pallets, and plant heads make decisions using outdated information.

At Weneura Futuretech, we engineer custom ERP software built around how your business actually runs. Because our team also works directly in robotics, sensors, and factory automation, our ERP connects straight to your weighbridge scales, digital counters, and machine PLCs. You get real-time shopfloor visibility, full source code and database ownership, and zero monthly per-seat licensing penalties as your team expands.

Core Custom ERP Modules

Bespoke modules engineered around your actual plant workflows, routing sequences, and operational scale.

Production & Routing

Multi-level Bill of Materials (BOM), work orders, job cards, machine stage tracking, operator dispatch logs, and scrap/rework analysis.

Inventory & Warehousing

Multi-warehouse stock, raw materials, WIP, and finished goods tracking. Lot/batch traceability, barcode/QR generation, and auto reorder triggers.

GST Accounts & Billing

Automated GST e-invoicing and e-way bill generation, job-work costing, customer ledgers, payables, and live P&L financial statements.

Procurement & Vendors

Purchase requisitions, vendor RFQs, purchase orders, and direct Goods Receipt Note (GRN) logging linked directly with digital weigh scales.

Quality Control & Traceability

Inward material inspections, stage-gate tolerance checklists, non-conformance logs (NCR), and end-to-end heat number traceability.

Hardware, Weighbridge & PLCs

Native direct connectivity linking digital weigh scales, weighbridges, handheld barcode/RFID scanners, and factory PLCs for real-time shopfloor telemetry.

Bridging Factory Hardware & Enterprise Software

Generic software agencies write web apps that stop at the keyboard. Weneura builds systems that bridge physical factory machines and digital business logic.

We connect your ERP directly to on-site sensors, weighbridge load cells, barcode scanners, and PLC controllers, eliminating manual data entry errors forever.
