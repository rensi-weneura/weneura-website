ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Engineering Industrial Robotics, Vision & Software. Together Tomorrow.

Weneura Futuretech Private Limited is an industrial technology and software company in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. We deliver robotics and automation, machine vision and AI inspection, industrial IoT and sensors, AI and machine learning, and software and ERP development (including Odoo) for manufacturers in India, the USA and the UAE.

We work with plant heads, operations teams and quality managers on real shopfloor problems: slow cycle times, manual inspection, missing production data and systems that don’t talk to each other. Every project starts with understanding your process, so the technology we recommend fits your plant and not the other way round.

Our team integrates industrial robots, builds camera-based inspection systems, connects sensors and PLCs to live production data, and develops custom software and Odoo ERP. Our founder, Siddharth Varsani, leads the company from Rajkot with one goal: practical technology that helps manufacturers produce more with less rework.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted industrial technology partner for manufacturers in India and abroad. We want every plant we work with to run with fewer manual steps, less rework and clearer production data, so teams can make decisions from facts and not guesswork.

We aim to bring robotics, machine vision, industrial IoT, AI and ERP together in one practical system, so a manufacturer doesn’t need a separate vendor for each layer. As Indian industry moves towards Industry 4.0, we want to make that shift affordable and dependable for small and mid-size plants, not only the largest ones.

Our Mission

Our mission is to design, build and support automation and software systems that solve real problems on the shopfloor. We build robotic cells, special purpose machines, vision-based inspection systems, sensor and data-logging solutions, and custom ERP, always starting from the manufacturer’s process and targets.

We work openly with our clients, from the first requirement discussion and feasibility study through design, installation and handover. We measure our work by what changes for you: shorter cycle times, less scrap and rework, and production data you can trust.

Our Values & Engineering Principles

Integrity & Confidentiality

We keep your drawings, process data and requirements confidential. Ownership of the work is agreed in writing before a project starts, so there are no surprises later.

Plant-Centric Automation

Your throughput, cycle time and quality targets guide our engineering. We design robotics, vision systems and software around the real constraints of your shopfloor.

Precision & Safety

We build for repeatable, predictable results: clean code, careful calibration and safety interlocks designed in from the start. We never trade safety for speed.

Practical AI & Vision

We keep up with machine vision, edge AI, collaborative robotics and industrial IoT, and we use them only where they solve a real problem in your plant.

Your Engineering Partner

We work closely with your plant and engineering teams and share progress, designs and decisions openly, so you always know where your project stands.

Accountability from Start to Finish

We take responsibility for the systems we deliver, from design and testing through installation and handover, and we stay available for support after go-live.
